# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Travellers booking tickets, operators managing buses and passengers, organizations managing fleets, and admins overseeing the platform.

## Product Purpose
India's premier bus booking platform. Safe, affordable, and always on time. Designed to provide speed and confidence to users booking travel.

## Brand Personality
Fast, reliable, intuitive. 
Reference: RedBus.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly complex, generic SaaS-heavy templates with decorative glassmorphism and cluttered metrics that distract from the core booking flow.

## Design Principles
1. **Optimize for Speed**: Reduce friction in the core flow from searching routes to final booking.
2. **Build for Confidence**: Use clear typography, transparent pricing, and robust error handling to make the user feel secure.
3. **Utility First**: Emphasize functional, intuitive interfaces over unnecessary decorative flourishes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No strict requirements specified yet; adhere to baseline best practices for contrast and clear, readable text.
